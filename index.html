01 · Data Handling

What happens to your data

Short version: it stays on your machine. The table below is the full picture.

DataWhat happens
Screenshots / captured imagesProcessed locally in your browser for OCR. Never uploaded.
OCR text / notesStored only in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) and as PNG files in your Downloads folder. Never transmitted.
Custom subjectsStored locally via chrome.storage. Never transmitted.
Usage analyticsNone. No analytics, no telemetry, no tracking scripts.

03 · Privacy Principles

Designed private by default

  • Local-first architecture — capture, OCR, search and storage all happen on-device.
  • Offline capable — the OCR engine ships inside the package; no runtime network calls.
  • Explicit translation only — pressing Translate opens Google Translate in a new tab, under Google's own policy. Never automatic.
  • No third-party SDKs — no advertising, crash-reporting, or analytics SDKs.

One exception, fully in your control: the optional Translate button sends your OCR text to Google Translate — but only when you click it. This is never automatic and never happens in the background.
